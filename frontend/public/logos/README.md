CoinGecko's `coingecko.svg` is the unmodified `CG-Symbol.svg` from the official
[CoinGecko brand kit](https://brand.coingecko.com/resources/brand-kit), downloaded
on 2026-10-06. Keep the brand symbol rather than substituting a Bitcoin icon.
The asset is served locally so viewing Services does not contact CoinGecko.

and filled with YouTube red (`#FF0000`). Source:
